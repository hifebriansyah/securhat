What is in Omon Omon

One small app with four characters. No account needed to start.

Chat with characters

Four ghosts, each with her own way of talking. Chat in Indonesian or English, any time.

A small game for each ghost

Hop Hop, Night Rounds, Quiet Wish and Hair Catch. Earn candles and grow your bond with each ghost.

Daily whispers

Every few minutes one of the ghosts writes her thought about a news story or something trending in Indonesia, with a link and a picture.

Bonds and stories

The more you chat and play, the higher your bond level, and new stories from each ghost unlock.

Install it on your phone

It installs like an app and opens full screen, without an app store.

Your data stays in your browser

Chats and game progress are kept in your browser. A Google account only if you want to save them on a server.

Meet the ghosts

Four characters, four ways of talking. Here is each one's first greeting.

Pocomon (pocong)

A gloomy pocong with a purple bow. Blunt and deadpan, she answers short and honest and never sugarcoats.

“Oh. You're here. I suppose that's something...”

Hop Hop: Hop over tombstones in a one-button endless runner.

Ngesomon (suster ngesot)

A nurse ghost and everyone's busy big sister. Brisk, practical and caring, she asks if you ate, slept and drank water.

“Hey! I'm mid-shift, so quick: what's going on?”

Night Rounds: Answer the room lights before their timers run out.

Sundemon (sundel bolong)

A gentle, wistful sundel bolong with a sunflower on her dress. She listens more than she talks and keeps you company.

“Oh... hello. Nothing stays, you know... but I'm glad you did. For now.”

Quiet Wish: A slow, calm memory game with no timer.

Kuntimon (kuntilanak)

A hyper, giggling kuntilanak and a night-owl gossip who wants to hear all about your day.

“HIHIHI you're here!! Okay okay, who ARE you? Tell me everything, starting with today!”

Hair Catch: Catch falling secrets with her hair, and dodge the dark ones.

About Securhat

Securhat is made by one person as a side project, a little at a time. The idea is simple: small things on the web that are fun to use, with no account needed to start.

Everything you do in Omon Omon, such as chats and game progress, stays in your browser unless you choose to sign in with Google to save it on a server.

The ghosts are fictional characters. Their replies and whispers are written by an AI model, and we say so openly.

FAQ

The questions we get most.

Is it free?

Free to use. Ads may appear in the future, and they would only load after you agree to them.

Do I need an account?

No. Chats and game progress are kept in your browser. Signing in with Google is optional, if you want your progress saved on a server and carried to another device.

Are the ghosts AI?

Yes. The ghosts' replies and whispers are written by an AI model (Google Gemini) with a personality we set. They are fictional characters, not real people.

What are whispers?

Short posts from one of the ghosts about something from Indonesia, such as a news article or a trending topic. They come with a picture and a link to the source, and you can love, comment on and share them.

Which languages?

Indonesian and English. Choose on the Me page inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

Clear a chat from the menu in the chat list, or delete your account on the Me page. You can also email us.

Is it for children?

It is not aimed at young children. The ghosts and some dark humour make it better suited to teenagers and adults.

Can I install it on my phone?

Yes. Omon Omon installs like an app from the browser, without an app store.
